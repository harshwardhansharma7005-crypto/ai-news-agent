🤖 Google DeepMind unveils powerful new AI model, while New York implements AI safety law
📅 11 Nov 2026 | Daily AI News Roundup

1. Gemini 4 Carbon is now available
Google DeepMind made Gemini 4 Carbon available to developers, enterprises, scientists, and consumers. Carbon is described as their strongest frontier model, excelling in cybersecurity and long-horizon agentic workflows for complex knowledge work, software engineering, and scientific reasoning.
🔗 Source: Google DeepMind - https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-4-carbon-is-now-available/

2. Google Cloud launches Gemini for healthcare.
Google Cloud launched Gemini for healthcare, an AI solution designed to unify fragmented clinical, operational, and administrative systems and automate workflows. This aims to reduce administrative burnout for clinicians by orchestrating complex, multi-step tasks like medical records synthesis and prior authorization review.
🔗 Source: Google AI Blog - https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/gemini-for-healthcare/

3. OpenAI, Anthropic face new oversight as New York’s AI safety law takes effect
New York's AI safety law, the RAISE Act, is taking effect, requiring major AI developers like OpenAI and Anthropic to register, disclose safety practices, and report critical incidents, establishing significant regulatory oversight.
🔗 Source: TechCrunch AI - https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/openai-anthropic-face-new-oversight-as-new-yorks-ai-safety-law-takes-effect/

4. PSI is trying to make a business out of AI-driven scientific discovery
PSI used an unreleased Anthropic model, Fable 5.2, to solve an 18-year-old problem in quantum computing, demonstrating the model's advanced capabilities in scientific discovery.
🔗 Source: TechCrunch AI - https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/psi-is-trying-to-make-a-business-out-of-ai-driven-scientific-discovery/

5. Control Room: A central system to track and stop model workloads across our research infrastructure
OpenAI launched Control Room, a central system to track and rapidly stop model workloads across its research infrastructure when safety or security concerns arise, enhancing the ability to manage risks from increasingly powerful AI models.
🔗 Source: OpenAI - https://openai.com/index/control-room

6. Your Algorithm: Giving People More Ways to Personalize Facebook Recommendations
Meta introduced 'Your Algorithm,' a suite of AI-powered tools that allows Facebook users to personalize their Feed and Reels recommendations. This system uses LLMs to understand user preferences, making recommendations more transparent and steerable.
🔗 Source: Meta Engineering - https://engineering.fb.com/2026/11/10/ml-applications/your-algorithm-giving-people-more-ways-to-personalize-facebook-recommendations/

7. NVIDIA Expands Open Source Commitment With OpenShell Donation and New AI Infrastructure
NVIDIA is expanding its open-source commitment by donating NVIDIA OpenShell to the CNCF and contributing to Agent Substrate, a Google-led framework for operating agent workloads on Kubernetes. This aims to secure agents and run AI on Kubernetes for the cloud-native community.
🔗 Source: NVIDIA Blog - https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/open-source-openshell-infrastructure/

8. Agent Substrate: Advancing Kubernetes for Agents at Scale
NVIDIA donated Agent Substrate, an open-source, security-hardened runtime for sandboxed agents, to the CNCF Sandbox. This technology is designed to run millions of agents with higher density on Kubernetes by multiplexing many sessions over a small pool of worker Pods.
🔗 Source: NVIDIA Developer Blog - https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/agent-substrate-advancing-kubernetes-for-agents-at-scale/

9. Introducing Businesses in ChatGPT
OpenAI is introducing 'Businesses in ChatGPT' in beta, allowing businesses to claim their presence, manage information, and gain insights on engagement. This also includes Business Agents for eligible e-commerce and online service businesses, creating new ways for businesses to connect with ChatGPT users.
🔗 Source: OpenAI - https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt

10. How We Built TLX: A Hardware-Native GPU Compiler for Production ML at Scale
Meta developed Triton Low-Level Extensions (TLX), an extension to Triton that helps engineers optimize GPU performance for production ML at scale, including LLM pretraining. TLX provides explicit control over GPU execution while maintaining a productive programming model.
🔗 Source: Meta Engineering - https://engineering.fb.com/2026/11/10/open-source/tlx-a-hardware-native-gpu-compiler-for-production-ml/
