🤖 SpaceX gets green light for 1 million AI satellites; Nvidia pauses chip shipments
📅 27 Nov 2026 | Daily AI News Roundup

1. FCC signs off on SpaceX’s plan to put up to 1 million data center satellites into space
The FCC has authorized SpaceX to deploy up to 1 million AI chip-equipped satellites for its 'Starmind' initiative, marking the first approval for a full constellation of AI satellites in space. This represents a significant step towards space-based AI infrastructure.
🔗 Source: The Verge AI - https://www.theverge.com/science/1042278/spacex-fcc-starmind-ai-space-data-centers

2. Nvidia hits pause on chip shipments to Southeast Asia
Nvidia has paused server shipments to Southeast Asian countries as the U.S. tightens export controls on AI chips to prevent them from reaching China. This impacts Nvidia's supply chain and highlights ongoing U.S. efforts to control AI technology access.
🔗 Source: TechCrunch AI - https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/26/nvidia-hits-pause-on-chip-shipments-to-southeast-asia/

3. OpenAI says heavy ChatGPT users may hit limits sooner as it merges Chat and Work
OpenAI is merging ChatGPT's Chat and Work experiences into a single usage meter, which may cause heavy users to hit limits sooner. This change could impact access to advanced models for some subscribers.
🔗 Source: TechCrunch AI - https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/openai-says-heavy-chatgpt-users-may-hit-limits-sooner-as-it-merges-chat-and-work/

4. Building on Nemotron 3.5 Parse: Open Data, Fine-Tuning, and Faster Inference
NVIDIA released new tools for Nemotron 3.5 Parse, a document model, including synthetic training datasets, a fine-tuning recipe, and a DFlash2 speculator for faster inference. This enhances the model's ability to extract structured data from documents for RAG and agents.
🔗 Source: Hugging Face Blog - https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/building-on-nemotron-35-parse-open-data-fine-tunin

5. How to Optimize NVFP4 Attention on NVIDIA RTX Blackwell Through Register Recycling
NVIDIA detailed how to optimize NVFP4 Attention on NVIDIA RTX Blackwell GPUs through register recycling, addressing attention bottlenecks in serving foundation models. This aims to accelerate attention beyond BF16 by leveraging hardware-accelerated 4-bit matrix multiplication.
🔗 Source: NVIDIA Developer Blog - https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/how-to-optimize-nvfp4-attention-on-nvidia-rtx-blackwell-through-register-recycling/
