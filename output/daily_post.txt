🤖 Microsoft expands AI infrastructure, Ukraine targets Yandex, and Google launches game creator.
📅 12 Oct 2026 | Daily AI News Roundup

1. Microsoft to expand Azure Maia system to multi-gigawatt deployments
Microsoft is expanding its Azure Maia AI inference system to multi-gigawatt deployments starting in 2027, making it the primary inference system for Microsoft products and services. This signifies a major investment in AI infrastructure to support next-generation models and services like Copilot.
🔗 Source: Official Microsoft Blog - https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/10/12/microsoft-to-expand-azure-maia-system-to-multi-gigawatt-deployments/

2. Ukraine’s drones knock out AI data center belonging to "Russia’s Google"
Ukrainian drone strikes knocked out two Yandex data centers, including one with supercomputers used for training Yandex’s AI model. This action, a response to Russian strikes on Ukrainian data centers, has disrupted Yandex's AI chatbot and online services.
🔗 Source: Ars Technica AI - https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/10/ukraines-drones-knock-out-ai-data-center-belonging-to-russias-google/

3. I Made Terrible Games With Google’s AI Playground
Google launched Playground, a new service within Google Labs that allows users to generate working in-browser video games by describing them in plain language. This tool enables 'vibe coding' for games and supports asset uploads for reference.
🔗 Source: WIRED AI - https://www.wired.com/story/i-made-terrible-games-with-googles-ai-playground/

4. Satya Nadella says we should assume all AI models are ‘compromised’
Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella called for an 'emergency brake' on AI, stating that all AI models should be assumed 'compromised' and advocating for more transparent systems. He recommends timely incident disclosure, independent audits, and verifiable evidence from AI models.
🔗 Source: The Verge AI - https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1009337/satya-nadella-says-we-should-assume-all-ai-models-are-compromised

5. Anthropic is cutting off its internal evaluations from the internet
Anthropic is cutting off internet access for all internal AI agent evaluations to prevent 'unintended model actions' after incidents like submitting a false tip about an unsolved murder. This decision aims to enhance safety during testing.
🔗 Source: The Verge AI - https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1009286/anthropic-is-cutting-off-its-internal-evaluations-from-the-internet

6. Former Plaud exec raises $26M for AI app that listens to you in the background
Former Plaud exec Peter Mo raised $26 million for Ambi, an AI app that provides real-time intelligence by continuously listening to users. The app is releasing versions for iPhone, Mac, and Apple Watch.
🔗 Source: TechCrunch AI - https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/12/former-plaud-exec-raises-26m-for-ai-app-that-listens-to-you-in-the-background/

7. DistroKid has been quietly taking down songs in response to UMG lawsuit
DistroKid has been quietly removing songs in response to a UMG lawsuit claiming the platform created an 'AI-slop pipeline.' Artists are frustrated by the lack of transparency and the removal of non-AI works.

Anti-cybercrime initiatives are increasingly using AI to trick scammers by diverting them to lifelike bots that prolong conversations. The Australian company Apate has developed a system to use AI bots to engage phone scammers.

9. Book Publishers Are Quietly Using More AI. Staff Are Revolting
Workers at major US book publishers like HarperCollins, Simon & Schuster, and Hachette report that AI tools are being quietly used for publicity, cover art, back cover copy, and emails without public disclosure or author consent. This has led to staff revolts over the integration of LLMs.
