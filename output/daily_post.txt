1. Nvidia hits pause on chip shipments to Southeast Asia
Nvidia paused server shipments to Southeast Asian countries like Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia to assess compliance with U.S. export requirements for AI chips. This action is part of the U.S. effort to prevent AI chips from reaching China through remote data centers.
🔗 Source: TechCrunch AI - https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/26/nvidia-hits-pause-on-chip-shipments-to-southeast-asia/

2. FCC signs off on SpaceX’s plan to put up to 1 million data center satellites into space
The FCC has authorized SpaceX to deploy up to 1 million AI data center satellites as part of its 'Starmind' initiative. This is the first approval for a full constellation of AI satellites, aiming to power AI capabilities on Earth.
🔗 Source: The Verge AI - https://www.theverge.com/science/1042278/spacex-fcc-starmind-ai-space-data-centers

3. A New Breed of Chip Runs AI Models Using Waves of Light
German company Q.ANT is developing photonic processors that use waves of light to run AI models, aiming to reduce energy consumption and cooling requirements. This new chip technology could address the ballooning energy costs associated with increasingly sophisticated AI models.
🔗 Source: WIRED AI - https://www.wired.com/story/a-new-breed-of-chip-runs-ai-models-using-waves-of-light/

4. Migrating our Messages API to Rust
Anthropic migrated its Messages API to Rust, using Claude agents to verify and fix differences between the Python and Rust versions. This demonstrates Claude's capability in complex code migration projects by leveraging its ability to verify solutions.
🔗 Source: Anthropic Research - https://www.anthropic.com/research/migrating-api-to-rust

5. 42 state AGs are trying to stop Character.AI from selling users’ chats
A coalition of 42 state attorneys general is attempting to prevent Character.AI from selling user data, including private chat histories of minors, during its Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings. This highlights significant concerns about data privacy and the protection of minors' information in AI services.
🔗 Source: The Verge AI - https://www.theverge.com/policy/1042620/character-ai-sale-state-ags-block-selling-users-chats

6. S&P considers Nvidia credit downgrade over governance and ‘key man’ risks
S&P Global Ratings is considering downgrading Nvidia's credit rating due to concerns about management, governance practices, and 'key man risk' related to CEO Jensen Huang. A downgrade could increase Nvidia's borrowing costs and impact financing for AI infrastructure.
🔗 Source: TechCrunch AI - https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/sp-considers-nvidia-credit-downgrade-over-governance-and-key-man-risks/

7. OpenAI says heavy ChatGPT users may hit limits sooner as it merges Chat and Work
OpenAI is merging ChatGPT's Chat and Work experiences into a single usage meter, which may cause some heavy users to hit limits sooner. This change aims to unify the user experience but has raised concerns among subscribers about reduced access to advanced models.
🔗 Source: TechCrunch AI - https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/openai-says-heavy-chatgpt-users-may-hit-limits-sooner-as-it-merges-chat-and-work/

🔗 Source: WIRED AI - https://www.wired.com/story/meta-refused-to-share-muse-for-testing-uk-financial-watchdog-complains/
