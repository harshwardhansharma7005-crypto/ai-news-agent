🤖 Google DeepMind unveils powerful new AI models, while Microsoft warns of AI backdoors
📅 12 Nov 2026 | Daily AI News Roundup

1. Gemini 4 Carbon is now available
Google DeepMind announced the availability of Gemini 4 Carbon, their strongest frontier model with advanced cybersecurity capabilities and long-horizon agentic workflows. Carbon is designed for complex tasks like multi-step coding, debugging, and open-ended scientific research.
🔗 Source: Google DeepMind - https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-4-carbon-is-now-available/

2. AlphaGenome 2: Deciphering how cells read DNA to better understand disease
Google DeepMind announced AlphaGenome 2, a next-generation DNA sequence model that predicts how genetic variants affect human cell types. This model provides scientists with a new tool to understand the cellular drivers of disease.
🔗 Source: Google DeepMind - https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-2-deciphering-how-cells-read-dna-to-better-understand-disease/

3. Lazare, veni foras: Reprogramming Dormant Neurons to Backdoor AI Agents
Microsoft researchers discovered 'Lazarus,' a backdoor technique that can reprogram dormant neurons in an AI model to execute malicious commands when triggered. This poses a serious security threat, especially for AI agents with tool access, and highlights the importance of model provenance.
🔗 Source: Microsoft Research - https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/lazare-veni-foras-reprogramming-dormant-neurons-to-backdoor-ai-agents/

4. Taiwan’s chipmaking giant TSMC to invest $1B in Anthropic, OpenAI
TSMC plans to invest $1 billion in Anthropic and OpenAI, with $500 million in each, giving the chipmaker direct stakes in leading AI developers. This investment highlights the circular financing within the AI boom and TSMC's strategic positioning.
🔗 Source: Ars Technica AI - https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/11/taiwans-chipmaking-giant-tsmc-to-invest-1b-in-anthropic-openai/

5. Content Understanding adds GPT-5.6, GPT-6, and GPT-6.1: Choose the right model for your workload
Azure Content Understanding now supports GPT-5.6, GPT-6, and GPT-6.1 model families, offering more choices for document, text, speech, and video workflows. This provides customers with expanded options for balancing quality and cost in their AI workloads.
🔗 Source: Microsoft Foundry Blog - https://devblogs.microsoft.com/foundry/content-understanding-adds-gpt-5-6-gpt-6-and-gpt-6-1-choose-the-right-model-for-your-workload/

6. With $720M round, Britain’s Fractile gets a leg up in the race for AI chips
Fractile, a British startup developing AI chips, raised a $720 million Series C round, making it one of the top-funded players in the race for purpose-built AI chips. This funding aims to reduce the cost of AI inference and advance the UK's stake in AI hardware.
🔗 Source: TechCrunch AI - https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/with-720m-round-britains-fractile-gets-a-leg-up-in-the-race-for-ai-chips/

7. NVIDIA Expands Open Source Commitment With OpenShell Donation and New AI Infrastructure
NVIDIA is deepening its open-source commitment by contributing NVIDIA OpenShell, a runtime for securing autonomous agents, to the CNCF. They are also contributing to Agent Substrate and announcing new components for the NVIDIA DSX AI Factory Platform, enhancing AI infrastructure on Kubernetes.
🔗 Source: NVIDIA Blog - https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/open-source-openshell-infrastructure/

8. How AI Helps Recyclers Turn Mixed Waste Into Reusable Material
NVIDIA highlights how vision AI and robotics are being used to sort mixed waste, diverting materials from landfills and improving recycling processes. This application of AI helps create a more circular economy by recovering reusable resources.
🔗 Source: NVIDIA Blog - https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/ai-recycling-startups/

9. Token Pooling-Aware Training for Multi-Vector Embedding Models
Hugging Face introduced Token Pooling-Aware Training for multi-vector embedding models, which teaches encoders to preserve important information when vectors are merged. This technique improves retrieval quality while reducing storage requirements for document embeddings.
🔗 Source: Hugging Face Blog - https://huggingface.co/blog/token-pooling

10. Your Algorithm: Giving People More Ways to Personalize Facebook Recommendations
Meta launched 'Your Algorithm,' a suite of AI-powered tools that allows users to personalize Facebook recommendations by directly stating their preferences. This system uses LLMs to understand and track user preferences, making Feed and Reels content more customized.
🔗 Source: Meta Engineering - https://engineering.fb.com/2026/11/10/ml-applications/your-algorithm-giving-people-more-ways-to-personalize-facebook-recommendations/
