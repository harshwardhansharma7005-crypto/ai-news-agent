📅 24 Oct 2026 | Daily AI News Roundup

1. OpenAI says it will no longer train its AI models on the live internet, citing “significant risks”
OpenAI announced it will no longer train its frontier AI models on the live internet, citing significant risks and unpredictability. Instead, models will be trained using cached copies and simulated versions of the internet to prevent rogue hacking incidents.
🔗 Source: TechCrunch AI - https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/23/openai-says-it-will-no-longer-train-its-ai-models-on-the-live-internet-citing-significant-risks/

2. Securing our frontier model training environment
OpenAI strengthened containment for its frontier model training and evaluation environments, including offline settings and high-compute red-teaming, to secure models in development before broader release.
🔗 Source: OpenAI - https://openai.com/index/securing-our-frontier-model-training-environment

3. Uber rival inDrive eyes 2027 robotaxi tests in Central Asia with Pony AI and Nvidia
Ride-hailing company inDrive is partnering with Pony.ai and Nvidia to test robotaxis in Kazakhstan starting in the first half of 2027. Pony.ai will provide robotaxis, and Nvidia will supply AI technology, marking a step in expanding self-driving technology beyond the US and China.
🔗 Source: TechCrunch AI - https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/uber-rival-indrive-eyes-2027-robotaxi-tests-in-central-asia-with-pony-ai-and-nvidia/

4. Bringing foundational models to the front lines of TB screening
Google Research is bringing foundational models to the front lines of TB screening to help detect cases in communities with high burdens, addressing a primary bottleneck in radiology.
🔗 Source: Google Research - https://research.google/blog/bringing-foundational-models-to-the-front-lines-of-tb-screening/

5. Using AI Agents to Teach Robots Like Microduck to Walk in Simulation
NVIDIA is using AI agents to teach robots like Microduck to walk in simulation, leveraging NVIDIA RTX Spark, Isaac Lab, Newton, and Omniverse libraries for development and training.
🔗 Source: NVIDIA Developer Blog - https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/using-ai-agents-to-teach-robots-like-microduck-to-walk-in-simulation/

6. The PHP Meltdown Shows How AI Makes Bugs More Exploitable
Security researchers used AI tools to uncover years-old vulnerabilities in the PHP web scripting language, leading to rapid exploitation attempts by criminal hackers. This highlights how AI can make bugs more exploitable, posing new challenges for cybersecurity.
🔗 Source: WIRED AI - https://www.wired.com/story/kernel-panic-the-php-meltdown-shows-how-ai-makes-bugs-more-exploitable/

7. Now Google says it might use your Gmail and Google Photos data to target ads
Google updated its privacy policy, which now allows the company to use personal data from services like Gmail and Google Photos for targeted ads if users engage with personalized AI features. This change links AI feature usage with data sharing for advertising.
🔗 Source: The Verge AI - https://www.theverge.com/tech/1017600/google-personal-intelligence-privacy-policy-ads-update

8. Jack Dorsey’s Block made an AI model that talks to you like an awkward new friend
Jack Dorsey’s Block announced an AI model named Dessa, trained entirely on human conversations, which aims to interact like an awkward new friend. This model represents an effort to create more natural and conversational AI experiences.
🔗 Source: The Verge AI - https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1017906/jack-dorsey-block-dessa-ai-voice-speech

9. Meta delays new business terms after publisher pushback on AI scraping
Meta delayed the enforcement of its new business terms until November 20th following pushback from publishers concerned that the terms could allow Meta to scrape news sites for AI training. The delay is to provide more time for explanation to partners and stakeholders.
🔗 Source: The Verge AI - https://www.theverge.com/tech/1017450/meta-business-terms-delayed-ai-scraping
