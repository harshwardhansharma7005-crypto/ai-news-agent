1. Scale Agentic Coding Workflows with Meta’s Muse Spark Open on NVIDIA GB300 NVL72
Meta launched Muse Spark Open, an 816B mixture-of-experts (MoE) model optimized for agentic coding workflows, trained on NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 Blackwell Ultra. It offers high first-attempt accuracy, reliable tool calling, and deep codebase understanding with a 1M token context window.
