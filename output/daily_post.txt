1. Jensen Huang’s exit puts Nvidia’s support for open source in question
Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned, raising questions about the company's commitment to open-source AI and its Hugging Face acquisition. Huang was a strong advocate for open-source AI, and his departure could impact the open model community.
🔗 Source: TechCrunch AI - https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/jensen-huangs-exit-puts-nvidias-support-for-open-source-in-question/

2. Introducing Gemini 4 Hydrogen
Google introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a new fast and cost-efficient AI model built for reliable agentic workflows and everyday tasks. This model is now available across Google AI Studio, the Gemini API, Gemini for business, the Gemini app, Google Search, and Google Antigravity.
🔗 Source: Google DeepMind - https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/

3. Hugging Face CEO vows to keep fighting for open source AI after Jensen Huang’s exit
Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue vowed to continue fighting for open-source AI after Jensen Huang's exit from Nvidia, expressing sadness over the departure. This raises questions about Nvidia's $12.9 billion acquisition of Hugging Face and its future commitment to open source.
🔗 Source: TechCrunch AI - https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/hugging-face-ceo-vows-to-keep-fighting-for-open-source-ai-after-jensen-huangs-exit/

4. AlphaGenome 2: Deciphering how cells read DNA to better understand disease
Google DeepMind announced AlphaGenome 2, a next-generation DNA sequence model that predicts how genetic variants affect human cell types. This model provides a new tool for scientists to understand the cellular drivers of disease by bridging single-cell biology and human genetics.
🔗 Source: Google DeepMind - https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-2-deciphering-how-cells-read-dna-to-better-understand-disease/

5. Taiwan’s chipmaking giant TSMC to invest $1B in Anthropic, OpenAI
TSMC, a major chip manufacturer, plans to invest $1 billion in Anthropic and OpenAI, with $500 million in each company. This investment gives TSMC direct stakes in leading AI developers, even as its chipmaking business profits from the AI data center buildout.
🔗 Source: Ars Technica AI - https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/11/taiwans-chipmaking-giant-tsmc-to-invest-1b-in-anthropic-openai/

6. Supporting research on AI and children’s safety
OpenAI committed $2 million to research on AI and children's safety, supporting a scientific research network under the Coalition for Children’s Rights and Protection in the Age of AI. This initiative aims to develop tools to measure AI's risks and opportunities for children, informing safer products and policies.
🔗 Source: OpenAI - https://openai.com/index/children-first-declaration
