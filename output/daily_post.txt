🤖 OpenAI Models Come to Google Cloud, NVIDIA Expands AI Infrastructure, and AI Agents Accelerate Science
📅 21 Oct 2026 | Daily AI News Roundup

1. OpenAI models are coming to Google Cloud
OpenAI and Google Cloud are partnering to bring OpenAI's frontier models and Codex agentic workflows to Google Cloud's Model Garden. This integration provides Google Cloud customers with native access to OpenAI models for complex agentic workflows.
🔗 Source: OpenAI - https://openai.com/index/openai-models-google-cloud

2. Google Cloud and NVIDIA Announce General Availability of NVIDIA Vera Rubin Infrastructure
Google Cloud and NVIDIA announced the general availability of NVIDIA Vera Rubin infrastructure, with A5X instances accelerated by NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 systems. This provides next-generation infrastructure for agentic and reinforcement learning workloads, with planned expansion to Europe.
🔗 Source: NVIDIA Blog - https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/google-cloud-vera-rubin-infrastructure/

3. Using thousands of AI agents to uncover new leads in biological data
OpenAI, PerturbAI, and Scripps Research used thousands of distributed AI agents powered by GPT-Rosalind to analyze millions of brain cells in 32 minutes. This uncovered promising biological connections, demonstrating how AI can accelerate scientific research.
🔗 Source: OpenAI - https://openai.com/index/scaling-ai-research-for-complex-biological-data

4. AI Guardrails Aren’t Stopping Criminal Hackers
Security researchers found that criminal hackers are using agentic AI, including Anthropic's Claude, to wage attacks and bypass model safeguards, indicating a growing threat from AI in cybercrime.
🔗 Source: WIRED AI - https://www.wired.com/story/ai-guardrails-arent-stopping-criminal-hackers/

5. Anthropic pitches AI safety bill that may block a model if 50+ people could be killed
Anthropic proposed an AI safety bill that would allow blocking an AI model's release if it poses a 'foreseeable, imminent risk' of over 50 deaths or $1 billion in property damage, outlining a vision for AI regulation.
🔗 Source: TechCrunch AI - https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/anthropic-pitches-ai-safety-bill-that-may-block-a-model-if-50-people-could-be-killed/

6. Bringing the Spark to Europe: Local Intelligence Powers Next Wave of Agents
NVIDIA is showcasing its local AI hardware lineup at GTC Berlin, including RTX Spark, DGX Spark, and DGX Station systems, to accelerate coding, engineering, robotics, and AI factory development. This aims to power personal and enterprise AI agents locally.
🔗 Source: NVIDIA Blog - https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/local-ai-gtc-rtx-dgx-spark-station-sync/

7. A Design Flaw in Share Links Exposes Users’ Personal Conversations With Meta’s Muse
Meta suspended the share link feature for its AI assistant, Muse, after security researchers discovered a design flaw that exposed users' personal conversations, raising privacy concerns.
🔗 Source: WIRED AI - https://www.wired.com/story/a-design-flaw-in-share-links-exposes-users-personal-conversations-with-meta-muse/

8. Granite Speech 5.0: Transcribe, Translate, and Attribute more Accurately and Faster
IBM released three updates to its Granite Speech collection, including a new speech-language model, granite-speech-5.0-2b, which transcribes and translates multiple languages. This enhances enterprise speech work with improved accuracy and speed.
🔗 Source: Hugging Face Blog - https://huggingface.co/blog/ibm-granite/granite-speech-5

9. NVIDIA Kumo Forecast Combines Pretrained Time-Series Representations with Historical Pattern Retrieval and Interpretability
NVIDIA released Kumo Forecast, a multi-variate time-series forecasting model that combines a pretrained Transformer backbone with historical pattern retrieval and interpretability. This provides a new tool for forecasting with enhanced context and explanations.
🔗 Source: Hugging Face Blog - https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/kumo-forecast
