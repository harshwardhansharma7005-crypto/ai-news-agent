🔗 Source: TechCrunch AI - https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/opera-introduces-a-new-web-standard-to-let-users-deploy-ai-locally-in-their-browser/

Nvidia has invested in Simplismart, an Indian startup that develops software to help companies run AI models more efficiently and with fewer GPUs. This investment addresses the challenge of economically running AI models at scale amidst the AI boom.
🔗 Source: TechCrunch AI - https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/nvidia-backs-indian-startup-helping-companies-run-ai-models-with-fewer-gpus/

NVIDIA is detailing three techniques to boost Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) inference performance on NVIDIA DGX Spark, focusing on reducing weight traffic and generating more tokens per verification round. This aims to improve the speed of token generation for AI models.
